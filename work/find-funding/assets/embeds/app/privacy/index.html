Privacy

Effective 2026-08-25

What we do with what you give us, in the plainest words we can write.

How we use AI on your information

AI processing. We use artificial-intelligence services run by third-party providers to read and organise what you give us: documents you upload, answers you type, and public records about your business. The provider we use for that work today is OpenAI; we may use others, the list can change as we build, and what we use for your documents is set out at /fine-print/documents.

We use these providers under subscription accounts rather than business data-processing contracts, so we have no retention terms and no no-training terms of our own to show you, and we are not going to print a number here that we cannot stand behind. We cannot audit a provider’s systems and we do not promise you what any provider does inside them. If we obtain written terms, we will say so here with the date we obtained them.

We send these providers only what is needed to do the work in front of us. We do not sell your information, and we do not send it to a lender or any other funder unless you pick that funder and say yes.

Addendum: Dev Mode

findfunding.ai is still being built, and while we build it we run many AI models from many different companies, switching between them as we test. Some of what passes through those models may end up being used to train them. We don’t know, and we won’t pretend otherwise. We have made our best efforts to keep your information private and secure even while we test, and if you would rather nothing of yours goes through them yet, type your figures in instead of uploading, or write to us and we will delete what you have given us.

What findfunding.ai is not

findfunding.ai is not a lender or a broker. We do not make credit decisions, quote rates, or promise approval. Any grant, loan or offer shown is one you may apply for; the funder decides.

What you are asked to consent to

When you upload a document, this is the consent you give:

To fill in your profile, the text of this document is sent to OpenAI, an outside company whose AI model reads it and returns the figures it finds. We mask Social Security, bank account and card numbers before we send, and we check every figure the model returns against your document so nothing invented reaches your profile. Your EIN, your business and personal names, addresses, and the financial figures on the page are sent as they are printed. We send no name, account or file name of ours with it. We use OpenAI under a subscription account, not a business data-processing contract, so we cannot promise you how long they keep what we send or whether it is used to improve their models; we tell you that plainly rather than quote you a number we cannot stand behind. Your document is held on our own server, reachable only by this service and the people who run it. You see everything we extract before it goes anywhere, nothing is shared with a lender unless you choose one and say yes, and uploading a document does not mean you have applied for anything or been approved for anything. If you would rather no outside company read it, you can skip the upload and type your figures in yourself.

When we preload public records about your business, this is added to that consent:

…and I understand AI services run by third-party providers, today OpenAI, may be used to read and organise those records, on the terms set out at /fine-print/documents.

Alpha Edition

findfunding.ai is an invite-only private Alpha Edition. The terms of that alpha (what unfinished software means, how AI reads what you give us, and that nothing here is an approval) are set out in the Alpha Edition waiver, reproduced in full in our Terms. Please read it; it describes the deal you are in.

What we collect

Four kinds of information reach us, and you can see all four.

What you give us to open an account. Your email address, a username, a password we store only as a scrambled value we cannot read back, and your name if you choose to add it.

What you tell us about your business. Its legal name and trading name, where it is, how it is set up, how long it has been going, roughly how many people work there, its revenue and other figures you type in, what you want the money for, and identifiers like a NAICS code or a SAM UEI if you have them.

Some funding programs are set aside for businesses with particular ownership, for example veteran-owned, woman-owned or minority-owned. There is a field where you can tell us. We can also end up holding the same thing without you filling that field in: from a certification you add to your profile, or from words in your business name, your web address or your profile keywords that say the business is owned that way. However we come to hold it, we use it to check the ownership criteria that set-aside programs publish, and to decide whether to ask you about it. It changes nothing about a program that does not publish an ownership criterion. Where a program does publish one, this is what we check you against, so it can change where that program sits in your list. You can leave it blank and still use everything here.

Documents you upload. Tax returns, profit and loss statements, bank statements, and anything else you choose to send us. We read them to fill in your profile, we show you every value we pulled out and the page it came from, and nothing goes onto your profile until you confirm it.

Public records about your business. Before you arrive and while you use the site we look at information that is already public, for example a company registration or your own website, so you are not retyping what the world already knows.

How the site is used. Ordinary technical records that any website keeps: the pages requested, the time, the browser, and the network address the request came from. We use these to keep the site working and to stop abuse.

How long we keep it

While your account is open we keep what you have given us, because that is the thing the site is for.

A file you upload but do not keep is discarded when you say so, and the file itself is gone.

A file you have kept and then delete has its contents and every fact read from it destroyed. A short record that a document existed and was deleted on a particular day survives so we can answer what happened.

We also keep a record of the consents you gave, including the exact wording you were shown and when. We keep those after the thing they authorised is gone, because that record is the only way either of us can later show what you were actually told.

During the alpha nothing is deleted automatically on a timer. We have not set a fixed period, and rather than publish a number we do not enforce we are telling you plainly that there is not one yet. When we set one, it will appear here and we will tell you before it starts.

Ask us to delete your account and what is in it, and we will. Write to the address at the bottom of this page.

If the alpha ends, we will tell you before anything is removed and give you a way to take your information with you first.

Your choices, and asking us for things

You can see and change what is on your profile at any time.

Nothing a document says lands on your profile by itself. Every value we read out is shown to you first, with the page it came from, and you confirm it, correct it, or reject it.

You can delete any document you uploaded.

You can decline to answer any question. The only cost of leaving something blank is that we cannot check the programs that depend on it, and we will tell you which ones those are rather than quietly dropping them off your list.

You can ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it and close your account. Write to the address at the bottom of this page and a person will answer.

If we email you about funding matches, every message has an unsubscribe link, it works, and we act on it. You can also just tell us to stop.

Nothing about you goes to a lender, a grantor, or any other funder unless you pick that funder and say yes.

Cookies and analytics

We do not run analytics on you. There is no Google Analytics, no tag manager, no session recorder, no advertising pixel and no third-party tracker on this site, and a test in our build fails if anyone adds one.

We do not set tracking cookies. When you sign in, the token that keeps you signed in is kept in your browser's own local storage, not in a cookie.

Two kinds of cookie can still reach you and neither of them follows you around the internet. Our hosting provider sets cookies to keep the site running and to block abusive traffic. Where a page sits behind a sign-in gate, that gate sets a cookie so it remembers you got through.

We do not sell information about you, and we do not share it with advertisers.

Changes to this policy

This policy will change, because the product is still being built. The date at the top of the page is the date of the version you are reading.

When we change something that matters, we will say what changed rather than quietly posting a new version. Every earlier version stays in our records; write to us with the date you have in mind and we will send you the version that was in effect then.

We will not apply a new rule backwards to information you already gave us. If we ever want to use what we already hold in a way this policy did not describe when you gave it to us, we will ask you first and you can say no.

If you do not like a change, you can ask us to delete your account and what is in it.

Contact

Questions about anything on this page: support@findfunding.ai. Effective 2026-08-25.
