Correctness spine — verification workbench

Every rule the matcher enforces, with its authority and human sign-off state. New rules registered in code appear here automatically as unverified. Attention first: expired → unverified → expiring → verified.

Match correctness — false-positive rate

A false-eligible is a case the harness says MUST be blocked that the engine cleared — a founder told they qualify for something they do not. Re-run every release. A failed or stale read shows as failed or stale; zero is never displayed unless it was measured.

Nightly health

Every night-nurse pass, newest first — severity, what it healed, what needs an operator. This is how operational health reaches a human without tailing logs.

Self-healing ledger

The post-sweep remediation pass: what failed, what the system retried, what recovered on its own — and the escalation list that actually needs you.

Ingestion coverage

Per-source extraction completeness, and the regression detector that flags a source whose coverage collapses against its own trailing baseline.

A low % is not automatically a bug (forecasts and rolling FOAs genuinely omit fields) — a sudden drop is a scraper regression, and the detector above owns that call.

Demand signals

What founders actually ask the engine for — prioritizes rule verification and roadmap; never alters a verdict.

Mining backlog

Sources flagged for rule mining that the night nurse hasn’t consumed yet — a growing backlog means the mining loop needs attention.
