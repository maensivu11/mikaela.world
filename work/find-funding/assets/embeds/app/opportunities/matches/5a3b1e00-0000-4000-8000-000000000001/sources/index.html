Sources & verification

How a finding is verified

Every stacking finding in your plan rests on published law, and each one carries its verification status and a link to the primary source. A citation names the rulebook it comes from: CFR is the Code of Federal Regulations and USC is the United States Code. So 2 CFR 200.403 and 10 USC 4022 each point at one numbered rule you can open and read for yourself.

Opportunity data is drawn from federal sources including SAM (System for Award Management) via sam.gov, Grants.gov, the National Institutes of Health (NIH RePORTER), and the National Science Foundation (NSF), and refreshes nightly. Deadlines shown as unknown are stated as unknown, verify at the source before relying on them.

The full citation list, rule-by-rule verification status, and the conditions attached to your stack are included in the exported plan (Prepare report, in the footer of your plan).

findfunding.ai helps you research. This is not legal advice and not a legal decision about your eligibility. We show the rules we found, with a source for each, and where they might conflict. Have a lawyer check any funding decision before you rely on it.
