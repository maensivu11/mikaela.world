What this software is, what it is not, and the rules we run it by.

What the AI produces

Uploaded documents are read by automated analysis to fill your profile.

What the AI produces. Our software uses AI to read your documents and draft summaries, fields, and matches. AI systems make mistakes, including confident ones. Everything the software fills in is shown to you before it is used or sent anywhere, and you are responsible for checking it. Nothing the AI produces is advice (not legal, tax, accounting, or credit advice), and nothing it produces is an offer, an approval, or a decision by any lender or grantor.

What findfunding.ai is not

findfunding.ai is not a lender or a broker. We do not make credit decisions, quote rates, or promise approval. Any grant, loan or offer shown is one you may apply for; the funder decides.

Estimates, not offers

Estimated fit, not an offer or approval. Based only on what you told us and this lender’s published criteria. The lender decides after its own review; terms may differ.

findfunding.ai Alpha Edition: what I’m agreeing to.

I’m accepting an invitation to the private alpha of findfunding.ai. I understand this is unfinished software: it will have bugs, it may lose or mangle data, features will change, and it may be taken down without notice.

I understand findfunding.ai uses AI services run by third-party providers, today OpenAI, to read and organise what I give it: the documents I upload, what I type, and public records about my business. I understand findfunding.ai uses those providers under subscription accounts rather than business data-processing contracts, so it has no retention terms and no no-training terms of its own to show me, and that it cannot guarantee what any provider does inside its own systems. My information is handled as described in the privacy policy, which I have read.

I understand AI output can be wrong, that I am shown what was extracted before it is used, and that checking it is on me.

Your account

You need an account to use findfunding.ai, and the account is yours. Keep the password to yourself, and tell us straight away if you think someone else has got into it.

Give us real information. Everything the site does for you is built on what you tell it about your business, so a wrong answer produces a wrong result, and a set-aside program checked against an ownership answer that is not true is a problem for you and not for us.

findfunding.ai is for finding funding for a business. It is not a place to look for a personal loan, a mortgage, or credit for household or family use.

You should be old enough to enter into a contract where you live, and you should be entitled to act for the business you are searching on behalf of.

One account is one person. If two people at the same business each want in, each of you should have your own.

What you may and may not do here

Use the site for its purpose: finding funding you might actually apply for.

Do not upload documents that are not yours to upload, or anyone else's financial information without their say-so.

Do not scrape the site, copy the funding database out of it, or resell what you find here as your own product.

Do not try to get into other people's accounts or other people's data, and do not probe, overload, or attack the service.

Do not use the site to break the law, to defraud a funder, or to put false information in front of a lender or a grant program.

If any of that happens we can suspend or close the account, and if it is serious we will do it without warning. Where we can give you notice first, we will.

We built this for small businesses and we run it for small businesses. If something here is getting in your way, write to us before you work around it.

Fees and referrals

The alpha is free. There is no charge to use findfunding.ai today: no subscription, no fee per application, and no cut of anything you raise.

We are not paid by funders to show you their programs. No lender, bank, or grant program pays us to appear in your results, and none of them pays for where they appear. What you see is what we found, in the order we think fits you.

That is true today and we will not change it quietly. If we ever take money from a funder in connection with sending you to them, we will tell you on the screen where it matters, before you act on it, and we will say who pays us and what for.

If we ever start charging you, we will tell you what it costs before you owe anything, and using the site up to that point will never create a bill you did not agree to.

We are not a broker and we do not take a success fee. We will not charge you a percentage of funding you receive.

Pausing or closing your account

You can stop using findfunding.ai whenever you like, and you can ask us to close your account and delete what is in it. Write to the address at the bottom of this page.

We can suspend or close an account if it is being used in one of the ways the previous section rules out, if we are required to, or if we stop running the service.

Except where something serious or unlawful is happening, we will tell you before we close your account and give you a chance to get your information out.

This is unfinished software in an early alpha. We may change it, take features away, or stop running it. If we shut it down, we will give you notice and a way to take your information with you.

Closing your account ends your access. What we keep afterwards, and for how long, is described in the privacy policy.

Who is responsible if something goes wrong

findfunding.ai shows you funding programs you might fit and helps you get organised to apply. It does not decide anything. The funder decides, every time.

Nothing here is legal, tax, accounting, or financial advice, and nothing here is an offer, an approval, or a promise of money.

The software uses AI, AI gets things wrong, and it gets things wrong confidently. We show you what it read and where it read it, and checking it before you rely on it is on you.

This is an alpha. It will have bugs, it may lose or mangle what you put in it, and it can go down without warning. We are telling you that plainly rather than burying it.
